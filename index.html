About Me

I’ve loved trying new things for as long as I can remember. Every new field, every new activity — there’s always something new to learn, and that’s what makes trying new things so exciting and fun for me.

Since elementary school, I’ve enjoyed seeing what I could do with a ball, a bike, or a board. I’m usually a little nervous the first time I try something new, but that’s part of the fun. I like coming back for another try and seeing what I can do a little better.

In the fall of 2027, I hope to start ninth grade at a school in the United States where I can take challenging classes, explore new subjects, and learn alongside classmates with different experiences. I want to work hard in class, keep playing sports, and be someone my classmates and teammates can count on. By the time I graduate, I hope to have the study habits and confidence to get into a college where I can pursue the interests I discover in high school.

My Family

Mom

Architect · Founder & CEO

Studied architecture in France, then worked as an interior designer at Shinsegae Group, where she worked with imported brands like Tiffany, and later with the U.S. fashion company Jones Apparel Group on its interiors in Korea. Today she runs her own design company, creating the big, busy spaces people love — resorts, hotels, food courts, and golf courses. When my friends walked into one of the resorts and said, “This place is amazing,” I got to tell them it was my mom’s work. She has a way of doing the things people say can’t be done.

Dad

News Team Leader · Korean Journalist Award

Studied business, then passed one of Korea’s toughest journalism exams and started out as a reporter at SBS. His reporting earned him a spot at his current company, where he now leads the news team — and he has won the Korean Journalist Award, one of the highest honors in Korean journalism. I know how many years of hard work went into it, so I was just as happy about his award as about my own. He is a news reporter whom millions of Koreans watch every day. I am so proud of him. He is my hero and my role model.

My parents work hard, enjoy life, and have given me every chance — a good education, the sports I love, even our travels together. Being their son is what I’m most proud of. I hope to learn from a wider world in the United States, and one day become someone they can be proud of, too.

Awards & Certificates

Beyond Sports: Awards, Leadership & Recognition. A look at achievements beyond athletics, including honors, leadership experiences, and certifications. (Korean certificates are presented with English translations.)

Honor Prize — 28th Korea National Students’ English Speech Contest

World Association for Arts Exchange · sponsored by Loyola Marymount University · July 15, 2023

A nationwide English speech contest that also selects student representatives for cultural performances in the United States. Ye-jun delivered his speech entirely in English and was awarded the Honor Prize (Usoo-sang, "Excellence Award"), one of the contest’s top recognitions.

Swimming Proficiency — Level 7 Certified

Korea Survival Swimming Association · December 1, 2023

Official survival-swimming certification issued by the Korea Survival Swimming Association. Passing Level 7 requires demonstrating sustained freestyle swimming and open-water survival skills — water confidence that carries into his water sports: water ski, scuba, and paddleboarding.

Class Vice President — Official Appointment

Chung-Ang University Middle School · March 4, 2024

Certificate of Appointment (Immyeong-jang) signed by the school principal: Ye-jun served as Class Vice President of Grade 7, Class 8, for the first semester of 2024 — a leadership role representing his class and supporting school events.

Encouragement Award — 20th TESOM National Math Assessment

Visang Education Co. · July 9, 2024

TESOM is a nationwide mathematics achievement evaluation hosted by Visang Education, one of Korea’s largest education companies. Ye-jun received the Encouragement Award in the first-half 2024 round as a 7th grader.

Grand Prize — Reading Certification Contest

Chung-Ang University Elementary School · December 12, 2023

Top Excellence Award (Choewoosoo-sang, the contest’s highest honor) in “Reading that Enriches Life”, the elementary school’s reading certification contest — earned as a 6th grader.

Hanja Proficiency — Pre-Level 5 (Jun-5)

Hanja Education Promotion Association · February 7, 2023 · lifetime validity

National Chinese-character (Hanja) proficiency certificate. Korean Hanja exam levels run from 8 (entry) down to 1 (expert): Pre-Level 5 (Jun-5) sits above Level 6, certifying command of roughly 1,500 characters — issued under Korea’s Framework Act on Qualifications.

Hanja Proficiency — Level 6 (6th Grade)

Hanja Education Promotion Association · February 15, 2022 · lifetime validity

His first national Hanja qualification: Level 6 (6th Grade), certifying practical knowledge of approximately 1,000 Chinese characters. He advanced to Pre-Level 5 within a year (see the certificate above) — steady, test-measurable progress.
